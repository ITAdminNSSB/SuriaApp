Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Gemba Walk? +

A Gemba Walk encourages employees to walk more, observe actual work processes, and identify opportunities for improvement while promoting a more active and healthier lifestyle.

How are winners selected? +

Main prizes: Male Individual Champion, Female Individual Champion and one BMI Category Gemba Champion. Team prizes go to Top 1, Top 2 and Top 3 teams. If scores are tied, the earlier score-achievement time ranks higher.

What happens if I scan a checkpoint out of order? +

The system will reject the scan and notify you to proceed to the correct previous checkpoint. You will not lose points, but you cannot progress until the sequence is corrected.

Can I change from Individual to Team later? +

Yes. Everyone remains an individual participant. You may also create or join a team of 2–5 members. Approval is required before team membership becomes official.

Can someone add me to a team without asking? +

No. Entering an Employee ID only sends an invitation. You must complete your gender, height and weight profile and accept the invitation before you become an official team member. A team requires at least 2 members. Member approval is required before team membership becomes official.

Do I need BMI verification now? +

No. Submit your height and weight and continue Gemba. Only shortlisted BMI Category candidates will be contacted for Office Lobby measurement.

Is the BMI Category leaderboard final? +

No. BMI Category ranking is based on Gemba points and remains provisional until Office Lobby verification is completed.

Can I continue my progress tomorrow? +

No. You must complete all 6 checkpoints within the same day. Any incomplete progress will reset automatically at midnight.
