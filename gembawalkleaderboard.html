Quick Rules Underweight/Normal → Male/Female Individual • Overweight/Obesity → BMI Category • Team is optional for all BMI categories • Team score uses average points • BMI finalists require Office Lobby verification.
BMI Reference & Calculation Use your current height and weight to calculate your BMI, then refer to the table below.
Formula: BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ [Height (m) × Height (m)]
Example: 70 kg ÷ (1.70 × 1.70) = 24.2 → Normal
BMI Classification BMI Range Gemba Personal Category
Underweight < 18.5 Male / Female Individual
Normal 18.5 – 24.9 Male / Female Individual
Overweight 25.0 – 29.9 BMI Category
Obesity ≥ 30.0 BMI Category
Disclaimer: BMI is a general adult screening reference, not a medical diagnosis, and it does not directly distinguish body fat from muscle or bone mass. For Gemba Walk, BMI is used only to determine personal-category eligibility. Winners are ranked by Gemba points. Height, weight and exact BMI remain private; only shortlisted BMI Category finalists require Office Lobby verification.
